Instructions

  • Optional: Begin by generating a timeline using the input fields below.
  • To create a new node, click the button in the lower left corner after closing this window.
  • To draw connections between nodes, or clusters of nodes, click and drag from one of the "ports" (circles) on either side of the node.
  • To resize a node, click it once and drag the handles of the now visible transformation box.
  • To delete a node, either press the "X" button visible when hovering the node, or select the node and press "Backspace".
  • To delete connectors, click the connector once and press "Backspace".
